# Exploration and looking around

- Targets are the use locations the scene has turned on, plus the scene's places. Availability comes from the native hook `UseLocationSMG026:ClientStateChanged` (0 Disabled, 1 Available, 2 LockedToUser, 3 Activated). The pawn's `AvailableUseLocations` and `OverlappingUseLocations` hold whatever box the character overlaps, turned on or not. The state flickers while the character stands in a box, so availability keeps a grace period.
- The whole game has four use-location labels, all verbs (Talk, Interact, Inspect, Examine), and draws no words over them. A name comes from the `ExplorationDestinationSMG026` standing beside it (closest pair first, within 250 cm), from its `LocaleString`, or from the level actor's name read as words (English; accepted).
- `ExplorationDestinationSMG026` actors are touch-mode waypoints (bare points, no collision); their prompts never appear with keyboard or pad. Scenes move on through `TriggerVolumeSMG` volumes watched by `GFConditionInVolume`: the mod reads the live state's `Transitions` (reached from the flow action that turned a use location on, two outers up) and offers those volumes as "Way on", never by name. Transitions that lead back to the same state (interludes) are skipped, and a way near a named target is dropped. A volume's actor location is its brush pivot, which can lie outside the volume: test against its collision (`GetActorBounds`, `GetClosestPointOnCollision`).
- The game offers one use location at a time, `CurrentUseLocation`, the nearest of the boxes the character overlaps. Reached means offered, or inside the box with nothing offered; there is no distance rule. The route's goal is the use box (`CollisionComponent`), its middle first, then spots at 0.7 of its extents, because an actor's origin can sit inside scenery.
- Routes: `NavigationSystemV1::FindPathToLocationSynchronously`. `IsPartial()` is true whenever the goal is off the mesh, so a partial route is used like a whole one. `K2_ProjectPointToNavigation` picks the polygon nearest across in 2D, so try a vertical extent of 120 cm before the wide one, or a target upstairs snaps to the floor below. Closed gates (`NavigationBlocker`) are not carved into the mesh, so routes run through them.
- Walking: `AddMovementInput` does not move this game's characters (their locomotion runs on goals). The walk holds the game's own movement keys with `SendInput`, arrows before WASD because a screen reader stops speaking on letter keys, or, with a gamepad in use, pushes the left stick through the XInput detour so the prompts stay gamepad ones. Release the keys on every way out, and stop at once on any screen, mechanic, loss of control (`IsInLoco`) or movement by the player. Judge a stall by `GetCharacterLinearSpeed()`: the body turns before it moves.
- Bearings are relative to the camera, and the game cuts between fixed cameras, so every bearing can turn at once.
- Tarot cards are timed button prompts, not use locations. Binoculars have nothing to find unless the flow runs a static exploration.
- How a tarot card is revealed, from the 26 placements of the 22 cards in the flows (`research\tarot_table.py`, `tarot_siblings.py`, `tarot_reach.py` over `research\flowjson_all`): the scene's state machine reaches a `GFStateDiverge` that forks the exploration and, as a sibling `GFStateNestedStateMachine` named `Tarot<Card>`, the card's branch; the fork precedes every exploration state of that machine. The branch waits in a `GFStatePassThrough` whose transition carries `GFConditionInVolumeSMG026(CharacterName, TriggerVolumeName)` into `Tarot<Card>Cam` (`GFActionPromptForButtonPressSMG026`, Interaction, `Timeout` 2.5 s, the Fool 4.5 s, plus the camera level sequence), and on `GFConditionCheckActivityResult` into `Tarot<Card>Unlock` (`GFActionCollectTarotCardSMG026.TarotCard`, `GFActionHideActor` of the `PaperTarot<Card>_C` actor). A missed prompt loops back to the pass-through, except the Strength card in the lodge (Act 4; the transition is guarded by a flag the cam state raises, and failure ends the branch) and the Fool's second placement in the prologue bunker (`T_Scarecam_leftentrance`, guarded by two flags). The volumes are `TriggerVolumeSMG` actors registered as `T_Tarot<Card>` (`T_TarotJudgment`, `T_TarotWorld_2` registered as `T_TarotWorld`), one per card and two for the Hermit and the Judgement's two routes. That table came from the 36 exploration flows alone, and one card is not there: the Strength is collected inside the arrival cutscene of the chapter 4 lodge, in the scene's main flow (`lodgearrive_gameflow`, machine "NestedStateMachine" of its first nested state: the sequence `officereturn_allarriveatlodge_026`, then a `GFActionPromptForButtonPressSMG026` with the camera blend `officereturn_getnicktonurseofficeCAMBLEND`, then on the press the collect, `GFActionHideActor PaperTarotStrength` and `officereturn_allarriveatlodge_tarot_fade`; a miss just plays on, and nothing loops back). Andrey saw the prompt at 18:26 on 2026-09-22 with Dylan in `CinematicRunning`, before any exploration. The on-foot Strength placement in `lodgearrive_gameflow_Exploration_01` (a 2 by 2 m volume near the fee letter, Dylan, guarded by `f_VisitedStrength`) is dead content: no transition of the scene's main flow enters the state that would run that machine, and no logged chapter 4 session ever had that exploration. So collect actions must be searched in every flow asset (`grep -l -a GFActionCollectTarotCardSMG026` over `research\flows`), and each exploration flow checked for a way in: besides the exploration flows, the main flows of the lodge arrival (the Strength), the eleven `act<N>fortune_GameFlow_Setup` scenes (the readings, a collect after a `GFActionChoice`) and `Act_2_GameFlow` (a Hanged Man grant on the "Is Preview Build" switch, not the retail game) hold one.
- The mod finds the watched cards from the exploration it can see (`TarotVolumes`): the machine (`Outer`) of a state whose use location is on, its `States` that are nested state machines, their `NestedStateMachine` (a `GFStateMachine` outered to the machine, not to the nested state), and among all live `GFActionCollectTarotCardSMG026` the ones whose state's machine is one of those. `GFTransition` carries `SourceState` and `TargetState`; the machine's `Transitions` lists them all. The reveal states are the sources of the transitions into the collecting state, and the transitions into the reveal states carry the volume. A card is spent when `UCollectableSMG::IsUnlocked(WorldContextObject, InSaveType)` answers true for `Runthrough` (1), or when the paper card the collecting state hides (`GFActionHideActor.ActorName`, an actor of `PaperTarot_Parent_C`, drawn from level load: no `bHidden` in the maps) has `bHidden` set; `Persistent` (0) spans every playthrough and the card lies in the world again then. The game's executor keeps its active states natively, so these are the only readings of "the branch is waiting", and the log names which one answered. A branch whose in-volume ask names a character that is not the player's, while a live character of that name exists (`GFConditionInVolume.CharacterName` against the pawn's `ActorRegister`), is left out and logged as "asked of Dylan, not of the player's character": the active states are found through the use locations they turn on, and the dead lodge exploration of chapter 4 names `BP_letter`, which the lodge of chapter 10 turns on, so under Kaitlyn its Strength branch showed up as a card of chapter 10 (a collected one in the log of 2026-09-22 15:34; an uncollected one would have been offered as a place).
- A branch's asks are read from its first state, the first transition of each state in turn (`GFConditionInVolume` with `bNot`), and followed as the character moves (`FollowTarotSteps`, kept in `g_tarotSteps` across scans): the World's branch asks Laura into `T_TarotWorldOuter` (194 by 380 cm, the card's 110 by 380 cm volume plus a 75 cm band on the room side), then out of it, then into `T_TarotWorld`; every other branch asks for its volume alone. So Laura, who starts in the band, must step out of it before the card's volume counts, and a walk from the wall etchings (whose use box begins 11 cm outside the band, so the game offers it while the capsule still stands in the band) into the card's volume reveals nothing: seen in a log on 2026-09-22, "reached" with no prompt, then the prompt on the way back from the bent spoon. After a missed prompt every branch waits for the character out of the card's volume before it takes its asks again. A card is reached only with nothing but its own volume left to ask; until then `Aim` leads to the ask at hand (a volume's middle, or `ExitPoint` just outside the one the character stands in), and the walk starts its measure of progress over when the aim moves.
- The cam state of the World, the Empress, the Emperor, the Death and the Hierophant sets a bool (`b_lauradoingtarot` and the like) that the explore state's `GFActionMakeUseLocationAvailable.ActiveIf` reads: the use locations go to state 0 at the prompt and come back only when the branch clears the bool, after the collect, or after a miss once the character has left the card's volume. The list empties meanwhile (N and P then say there is nothing to walk to), and the targets come back as they were: `g_gone` remembers for 30 s what left the list while the player kept the character, so their return is not announced and the chosen one is chosen again.
- The cards' places are off by default (`[Exploration] TarotCards`, K or RB) and go by the same "Way on" words as the scene's ways. A place is not offered where the walked part of a road to a use location, a place or an offered way (`WalkedRoad`: cut where the game offers the use location, at the way's edge, or 150 cm from a place) comes well inside the card's volume, by the character's width, counting only from the first point of the road outside the volume (the test itself is against the volume's collision, see below): the World's box was hidden by a corner clip of the road to the wall etchings that no walk ever took. The hide sticks to the target whose road caused it (`g_cardHiddenBy`) and holds while that target is still listed, wherever the character goes: judged from the character's position alone, the Hierophant (a strip across the corridor to the attack marks) and the Sun were offered as new the moment the walk had crossed and revealed them, and the Death, on the way to the cabinet from the start, was offered once the character had walked past the cabinet to the side door. A toggle of the key judges the cards afresh. Verdicts are logged when they change. Both judges take only the stretches of a road at the volume's height (`AtHeight`: the floor point plus the capsule's height against the box's z range): the lodge of chapter 10 has three storeys (ground 5600, the dog tags' floor 5900, the attic 6200), the High Priestess's volume spans 6171 to 6371 over the dog tags' spot, and in two dimensions the road to the dog tags ran through it, so the card was hidden for the whole exploration except the six seconds after a toggle of the key (route 17 m from the dog tags, up through the attic). Per act: Prologue the Fool (woodland road, and again at the bunker's left entrance); 1 Temperance; 2 Hanged Man; 3 Star, Tower; 4 Strength, Magician; 5 Devil, Hermit; 6 Justice, Moon; 7 World, Chariot; 8 Empress, Emperor, Lovers, Wheel of Fortune; 9 Sun, Death, Judgement, Hierophant; 10 High Priestess.
- Looking around: `GFActionStaticExplorationSMG026.POIObjects` carry yaw and pitch tolerances, but the flow's own `GFConditionIsLookingAt(LookTarget, AngleDegrees)` is what really judges (the campfire photo: 20 degrees as a half angle; 16 degrees off counted). Guidance uses the `[Combat] AimSound` switch, and T toggles it. `MulticastPOIFound` is hooked but has never been seen firing.
- Eleven of the 26 card volumes are turned (Justice 37 degrees, the Tower 29, the Hermit 59, the Lovers 80, the Star 20, the Magician 19, the Devil 22, the Death, the Sun, the Emperor and the Judgement by a few), and `GetActorBounds` gives the axis-aligned bounds of a turned box, up to half again as large as the box. A road judged against those bounds is "through" a volume it never enters: Justice, off the road to the woods' exit by 348 cm, was hidden by it. So the cards are judged against the volume's own collision, sampled every 25 cm along the walked road (`PointInVolume`, the probe at the height the capsule reaches into the box), a sample counting only when the capsule stands wholly inside (the point and the four points a radius away), from the first sample outside the volume on (an offline check does the same with the box's frame from the map). The ways' own filter keeps the bounds: its volumes are axis-aligned and nothing has shown it wrong.
- The mesh the game walks on is not always the cooked tiles: the game runs `RuntimeGeneration=DynamicModifiersOnly` (`DefaultEngine.ini`; the persistent maps set nothing else), its own `NavigationSystemSMG`, `ANavigationBlocker` volumes that doors spawn and flows switch (`GFActionSetNavigationBlockerState`), and `GFActionSetMainNavMesh` naming the scene's map. In the woodland date the ground at the base of the trail cameras is an island of its own layer in the tiles, yet the game's road ends on it, complete. And the Tower (log of 2026-09-22 17:11): the mod offered it, walked into it and got the card, while every road the tiles give from Abigail's start to the trail cameras runs through the middle of its volume, the same roads the mod logged to within 7 cm and whose corners the walk log confirmed metre by metre; the volume's centre, size and turn are confirmed by the logged distance and bounds. Unexplained offline. The mod therefore logs, at Verbose, the walked roads it judges the cards by (`the road walked to "..." for the cards`), at each hand-over which navigation data answers and its runtime generation (`navigation by ...`), and in a card's verdict how near the road's samples came to the collision by the engine's own measure, so that a log settles it.
- A character handed back for the seconds it takes the game to leave for the main menu is not a scene to explore: while `features::LeavingTheGame`, the keys hint and the targets say nothing, however much the world looks like itself.
- Who has the character, from the game's code and flows. The scene's explore action (`GFActionExplore`, native, no UFunctions, no instance to read) takes the local player's character, never a named one, and marks it while it runs (a native byte of the character, set on start and cleared on exit). The character's cinematic state machine (`ECinematicState`) keeps it in Loco only while it is so marked, or while `GFActionSetLocomotionAuthorised` lets it walk, or when its `bLocomotionRequiresAuthorisation` is off; otherwise Loco falls to Standby on the next update. A cutscene goes Loco → LocoToCinematic → CinematicRunning → Unmount → CinematicToLoco (the blend back) → Loco. The getters answer one state each: `IsInLoco` Loco, `IsSleeping` Standby, `IsBound` Mount, `IsBoundAndRunning` CinematicRunning, `IsInCinematicToLoco` the blend. The game's own "use the stick to move" prompt comes only in Loco, under the explore action, with the world not paused.
- Every scene lets a character walk outside an explore state only as a companion walked by an AI (`GFActionAIPossessCharacter`, `GFActionAICommand*`; Abigail beside Nick, Emma beside Jacob, Ryan and Travis behind Laura) or together with the explore action; movie mode chains the cutscenes with neither. So for the player's pawn `IsInLoco` is the explore action's hold, and it drops the frame a cutscene or the end of the action takes the character. The mod reads it at every frame (`PollControl`), logs the cinematic state whenever it changes, plays the control cues on each change (silent while `features::LeavingTheGame`), and the roaming takes the change up on the same frame. A pause freezes the state, so the pause menu plays no cue.
- A logged session bears this out: the character went from Loco to CinematicRunning the frame after a use location was pressed (state 3), and back to Loco the frame after the scene turned its use locations on again, the game drawing its prompt some 50 ms later; every playable character had `bLocomotionRequiresAuthorisation` set; and between the quit popup and the main menu the pawn went away, the frontend holding none.
- The player walks the character while there is a pawn, no menu screen is current (`watch::CurrentScreen`), no game mechanic is shown and `IsInLoco` holds. Every change of the reason is logged (`not under the player's control: a screen is up, ...`). A screen held past its closing once kept exploration off for up to 75 s after a clue viewer, while the game had long handed the character back.
