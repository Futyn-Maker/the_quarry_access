# Choices, prompts, quick-time events, button mash, Don't Breathe

- The options in play are the HUD element's pointers: `ActionHUDChoiceSMG026.ChoiceAWidgetInstance`, `ChoiceBWidgetInstance`, `ChoiceTimeoutWidgetInstance` (null means not offered) and `ActionHUDMultiChoiceSMG026.ChoiceWidgetInstance[]`. The game detaches an unused option from its panel and leaves its flags and opacity alone.
- A two-way option (`ChoiceWidgetSMG026_C`) shows its `TitleText` label first and its `SubtitleText` line about half a second later: read the choice once when the lines are in. Key caps show only when `InfoDataInstance.bShowInputPrompt` is set (the `AxisInputPrompt`, one second in); never name a key the game does not display. `AxisInputPrompt.DisplayAxisInputType`: 0 none, 1 left stick, 2 right stick.
- A choice commits when the option's own `Info.CommitFraction` fills while held (`bChosen` is never set for four-way choices, and `CommitScalars` stays empty). Holding a letter key silences NVDA, which cuts speech on every auto-repeat of a typed character: feedback that follows a hold waits until `input::InputHeld()` turns false.
- Four-way layout: `ButtonPromptDown` is upper left, `ButtonPromptUp` upper right, `ButtonPromptLeft` lower left, `ButtonPromptRight` lower right. The sticks drive it (`TarotSelectionAxis*`), and so does the mouse (`bIsMouseHighlighted`, `bIsClicked`); the keyboard often does not.
- Context for one particular choice goes in the notes table of `Choices.cpp`, keyed by the option's locale key, and only when the scene shows it (the chapter 8 cage numbers are painted in the scene).
- Every mechanic ends the same way: a tone for what happened, and the word for it when the mod is detailed (`result.success`, `result.failure`, and the shot's own `combat.hit` and `combat.miss`). Quick-time events had only the tone, which made the one mechanic that says nothing about how it went; they say it now like the rest.
- A prompt the game puts back up while it takes the world apart around a player who is leaving it is not read (`features::LeavingTheGame`): answering the pause menu's quit popup hands the character, the interaction prompt and the walk keys back for a few seconds before the frontend comes up, and all of it is true of the screen and none of it is of use.
- Prompts: the hook on `InputActionInteractionPromptWidgetSMG026_C:SetButtonPrompt(ButtonInputMappingName, Labels, Style)` names the action. Styles: 0 Default, 1 Secondary, 2 STE, 3 UseLocation, 4 UseLocationHold, 5 Combat, 6 Interrupt. `$(prompt)` in a label is replaced with the key's name.
- An interruption (style 6) is marked by `Cue::Interrupt` in that hook, the earliest moment there is: in every logged case the timer bar and the prompt widget appeared 10 ms after it and the words were read 150 to 250 ms later, once the prompt had settled. The timer bar (`ActionHUDTimerBarSMG026`, `TimerBarWidgetSMG026`) appears beside an interruption or a fight and nothing else (67 of 67 appearances in the logs to 2026-09-22; a timed choice draws its own bar inside the choice widget), so its word "Timer." is kept for the fight at either verbosity and, for a bar raised within a second of an interruption's set-up (`InterruptPromptSetUpWithin`), left out at the brief level, the sound having said it.
- Quick-time events (`DirectionQTEViewportWidgetSMG026_C`): the accepted action comes from `GetActionMappingSuccess()`; the angle is 0 up, 90 right, 180 down, 270 left. The game takes the press only once the marker settles (about 0.8 seconds in), so the cue sounds again then, and the direction repeats every second until the result. Results come from the animation events `...SuccessAnim..._8` and `...FailureAnim..._9`, with `IsAnimationPlaying` polled as the twin. Primary and secondary displays show the same event: dedupe by angle and start time.
- Button mash: the widget has no text, only a glyph and a ring scaled by `CommitFraction`; its words come through the prompt widget. `ButtonMashModeSetting`: 0 Standard, 1 Hold, 2 Tap, 3 Auto. Button bursts use W, A, S, D and E.
- Don't Breathe: `GetDontBreatheInfo().DangerZones` are not drawn, but the game conveys them in picture and sound, so the end of a stretch of danger is announced (`Cue::Up`, "The danger has passed") and the beginning of a later one (`Cue::Down`, "Danger."), each when it happens and never ahead of it: a warning 1.5 s before a later stretch came while the player was rightly letting go in the gap between the two, and called danger where there was none (the log of 2026-09-22 15:51). The opening state is marked by the tone at the widget's appearance and said by the game's own prompt. The bars are the lungs and the zone is the creature, independent of each other: success is reaching the end of the danger with air left, not letting go at a clever moment. What the game's data holds (13 flows with a `GFActionDontBreatheSMG026`, 19 actions, `grep -rl -a` over `research\flows` and UAssetGUI): every table of stretches begins at 0 (one at the action's own `StartTime` of 5 s), 10 actions have one stretch and 9 have two, the second beginning 6 to 13 s after the first ends and running to the action's `Duration` in most of them. In every one of the 22 encounters the logs hold up to 2026-09-22, the release after the first stretch ended the action with success within 1.5 s, so the second stretch is met only by a player who holds on through the gap, whose breath the bars then run out inside it. The Bonus tutorial is a scripted demonstration that ignores input (`DontBreatheTutorialOverlay_C` or `TutorialVideo_C` on screen): say so and give no advice.
- When a pause screen closes, the game shows an answered choice's container again (`already decided`) and keeps it drawn for up to a minute. `ChoiceDecided` (an option with `bChosen`) tells it apart, so it counts as no mechanic; a new choice on the same widget clears `bChosen`.
