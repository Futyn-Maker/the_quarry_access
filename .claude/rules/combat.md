# Real-time combat

- `GFActionRealtimeCombatSMG026`: every target dies from one hit, and the flow judges a hit (sometimes per target), a miss after N shots, or a timeout. Story fights last 1.6 to 8 seconds, the chapter 2 shooting range 200. `CombatAimSetting`: 0 Off, 1 On (assist), 2 Auto (the game plays the fight; the mod says only "Aiming, automatic").
- There is no crosshair (`bEnableReticle` is never set): the aim is the weapon's torch beam. Aim input is `AimingX/Y`, the mouse or the right stick only.
- The shot, from the game's code: it starts at the muzzle socket (`Barrel_socket`) along the torch's rotation; 35 pellets become a uniform 5 by 5 grid 0.54 degrees apart (`bUniformSpread`); each pellet is a line trace on `TargetCollisionChannel` over `WeaponEffectiveRange`, and a blocking hit on a `PossibleTargets` actor is a hit. Trace every `PrimitiveComponent` of a target: a bottle's collision is a capsule and its mesh has none. Recoil kicks the torch after a shot, so only the line before it counts.
- Aim assist: a 60 cm sphere swept along the torch over `AimTargetDistance`; the nearest target it touches pulls the aim. `MinLockDistance` and `MaxLockDistance` (2 and 7) are a band in a 2D screen unit, not metres. The Off setting zeroes all of it. `TargetPos` on the replicator is the aim direction 80 m out, never a hit point.
- Targets: `PossibleTargets[].Target.ActorName`, resolved through `ActorRegister`, taking the nearest of same-named actors (the persistent level holds copies from other scenes). Never speak a target's name: the game hides identities in several fights. Say creature (the class or name holds "wolf"), object (not a pawn) or target, with distance and side. Among several targets, which one the sound leads to ("Creature 1 of 2") is a place in a list and is said at the detailed level only; the distance and side tell them apart at either.
- A new `ARealtimeCombatSMG026_Replicator` is spawned for each fight; its `Path` ends with the fight's state label, which picks the action, and the fight ends when the replicator is destroyed. The combat camera modifier is not a usable signal. A pause, a screen over the picture or the way out to the main menu stops the mod's fight rather than ending it (`FightHeld`): ended there, nothing begins it again, since the pause UI turns the camera modifiers off as it starts ("Start Pause Level", `GFActionPauseCameraModifierSMG026`) and on again in "Hide UI" while the world is still paused, and the fire prompt is not set up anew, so the aim fell silent for the rest of the fight. While the fight stands still nothing is judged, sounded or timed out, `CombatActive` answers false so its keys are the game's (the D-pad would otherwise also step through the targets in the pause menu), and on resume its clocks move on past the pause (`Resume`), so that the pause counts neither toward the time limit nor against a shot in flight. Only the replicator's end and the character's going end a fight that stands still, and such an end, the world taken apart or turned back, passes no verdict on the shot or the time.
- The double ping is the moment's truth (a pellet meets a body now). "Fire!" needs the grid to meet the body from the line swayed 0.15 degrees each way, held 0.2 seconds; with the stick still, aim sway moves the line by 0.1 to 0.2 degrees.
- The setup reads `FActorReference` strings, which can be garbage right after a death rewind, so it runs under `SafeInvokeLogged`.
